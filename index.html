Our Mission

At Twin Cities Animal Rescue, our mission is to provide a safe haven, medical care, and loving forever homes to animals in need. We believe every pet deserves a second chance and we strive to build a compassionate community through education and advocacy.

Our Impact

  • Over 500 pets successfully adopted last year
  • More than 1,200 animals fostered since 2018
  • 100% of our rescues receive full veterinary exams before adoption

How You Can Help

We rely heavily on the generous support of our community. You can help by adopting a pet, opening your home as a temporary foster, donating supplies, or volunteering your time at our rescue facility.

Adoption Process Overview

Finding your new best friend is easy. First, view our available pets online or visit our shelter. Next, complete a quick adoption application. Finally, meet with one of our adoption counselors to ensure it is a perfect, lasting match for your family.

Foster Program Overview

Fostering saves lives by opening up critical space in our shelter. We provide all the food, medical care, and supplies. You just need to provide a temporary, loving home until the pet is ready for adoption.

Volunteer Role Descriptions

  • Dog Walkers: Help our shelter dogs get their daily exercise and much-needed socialization.
  • Cat Cuddlers: Spend quiet time playing with and socializing our rescued cats.
  • Event Helpers: Assist at community outreach and mobile adoption events across the city.

Organization Story

Founded in 2018 by a dedicated group of animal lovers, Twin Cities Animal Rescue started as a small foster-based network. Today, we have grown into a community pillar, actively rescuing stray, abandoned, and surrendered animals throughout the metro area.

Core Values

  • Compassion and respect for all living creatures
  • Integrity and transparency in our daily operations
  • Commitment to finding the right home, not just any home

Partner Acknowledgments

We couldn't do this without our amazing community partners. A special thanks to City Vet Clinic for their discounted medical services and Paws & Claws Pet Store for their monthly food donations.
